Digital Sprouts of Egypt

The "Digital Sprouts of Egypt" initiative is a free program launched by the Ministry of Communications and Information Technology for students in the fourth to sixth grades of primary school to equip them with information and communication technology skills. The initiative includes courses in programming, artificial intelligence, and game development, in addition to life and leadership skills, through virtual and practical training sessions.

Goals of the Initiative

  • Preparing a digital generation: Equipping young people to foresee new horizons in ICT to meet future job market requirements
  • Skill refinement: Empowering students with ICT fundamentals and developing critical thinking and innovation skills
  • Achieving the digital vision: Contributing to Egypt's digital vision by building an integrated digital community

Target Audience

Students in the fourth, fifth, and sixth grades of primary school in all schools across the republic.

Training Content

  • Fourth grade: Programming methods
  • Fifth grade: Introduction to Artificial Intelligence
  • Sixth grade: Digital game development

Training Format

  • Weekly virtual sessions
  • Practical sessions once every term at designated training centers in the governorates
  • Trainees receive a completion certificate upon finishing the program

Required Documents

  • A computerized copy of the student's birth certificate
  • A registration letter from the school confirming enrollment
  • A clear photo of the guardian's national ID card
  • The official declaration signed by the guardian and the student (download from the website)
  • A personal photo of the student (4×6) with white background

Restrictions

  • Students who joined the main or summer program in previous batches cannot apply
  • Students who withdrew or were expelled cannot apply until one year has passed
