Türkiye Scholarships (Türkiye Burslari)

Türkiye Scholarships is a government-funded, competitive scholarship program awarded to outstanding students to pursue full-time or short-term programs at top universities in Turkey. Every year they select around 5,000 students from all around the world, making it one of the largest scholarship programs for international students.

What's Covered (Fully Funded)

  • University and program placement
  • Monthly stipend (Bachelor: 4,500 TL | Master's: 6,500 TL | PhD: 9,000 TL)
  • Tuition fees
  • One-time return flight ticket
  • Health insurance
  • Accommodation
  • One-year Turkish Language Course

Programs Available

The scholarship covers Bachelor's, Master's, and PhD programs in engineering, health sciences, humanities, social sciences, and natural sciences.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Undergraduate: Minimum 70% in high school
  • Master's & PhD: Minimum 75%
  • Medical Studies: Minimum 90%

Age Limits

  • Bachelor's: Under 21 years old
  • Master's: Under 30 years old

Application Deadline

Applications open every year from 10 January to 20 February.

How to Apply

Applications are made online through the official website. It's free and you must apply yourself.
