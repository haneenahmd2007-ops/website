To know us

Who are we?

We are an educational platform dedicated to global travel, where Future Passport offers international student competitions and scholarships at top universities worldwide. 🌍

When we started?

The Future Passport platform began in 2019, where we applied for more than 10,000 scholarships and competitions for students from all over the world. 😊

Our vision

Our goal is to help all students, guide them towards the future, facilitate all procedures for them, provide them with comfort and assistance, and for Future Passport to become the largest global platform for international competitions and scholarships worldwide. 🎓
