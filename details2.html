I Am Volunteer Program

The Ministry of Youth and Sports, in collaboration with the Ministry of Culture and the Egyptian General Book Organization, announces the opening of applications for the "I Am a Volunteer" program, the official volunteer program for the 57th Cairo International Book Fair, the largest cultural event in Egypt and the Middle East.

Goals and Benefits

  • Real practical experience: A 14-day government fieldwork experience (in two groups, each group for 7 days)
  • Strong experience that adds significant value to your resume and enhances scholarship acceptance opportunities
  • Certified government certificate: Official certificate from the Ministries of Youth and Sports and Culture
  • Comprehensive training: Online and offline training to raise professionalism and prepare young people to take on responsibilities

Logistical Advantages

  • Uniform
  • Daily meal
  • Daily transfers from specific points (Tahrir Square – Nasr City) to the exhibition venue at the Egypt International Exhibition Center

Volunteer Tasks (Choose One Committee)

1. Organizing and Information Committee:
Organizing the flow of visitor entry and directing them within the exhibition halls and pavilions.

2. Media Documentation Committee:
Coverage of the exhibition events with professional photos, videos, and daily editing.

Conditions for Joining

  • Age between 16 and 24 years
  • Ability to commit to 7 days of volunteering during the exhibition period (14 days)
  • Passing the selection stages: Electronic form, Online training course + exam, Personal interview

Application Stages

  • Online application followed by initial screening
  • Online training program via the Mentor platform
  • Electronic test on the Mentor platform (Second round)
  • Personal interviews at the Ministry of Youth and Sports
  • Advanced online training program for the selected group (Third selection)
  • Offline training program to complete the qualification
  • Cairo International Book Fair events begin

Event Dates

January 21, 2026 – February 3, 2026
